# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Knowledge workers, managers, sales teams, researchers, and operators who need to review meetings, find decisions, follow transcripts, and turn conversations into actions.

## Product Purpose

Provide a faithful, working Fireflies.ai-style meeting intelligence workspace. Success means users can browse seeded meetings, inspect synchronized transcript content, review summaries and action items, search globally, upload transcripts, and export meeting records through a production-quality API.

## Positioning

Meeting records become searchable, navigable work products instead of passive recordings.

## Brand Personality

Precise, efficient, dependable. The interface should feel like the current Fireflies.ai product: dense enough for repeated work, calm in neutral states, and clearly branded through purple AI and action accents.

## Anti-references

Do not reinterpret Fireflies.ai as a generic SaaS dashboard. Avoid decorative marketing layouts, oversized typography, excessive cards, invented navigation, glass effects, or visual changes that reduce fidelity to the reference product.

## Design Principles

- Fidelity first: current Fireflies.ai is the primary source of truth.
- Workflow continuity: navigation, search, meeting review, and transcript actions must connect.
- Familiar controls: standard product affordances, predictable states, and restrained motion.
- Information clarity: transcript, metadata, summaries, and actions remain easy to scan.
- Responsive structure: preserve workflow on narrow screens through deliberate collapse and stacking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve visible focus, and never communicate status through color alone.
